About GadgetVerse

Prices first. Evidence beside them.

GadgetVerse checks supported store listings when you compare a product. Verified prices are shown with the store and the time they were observed; unavailable listings stay marked unavailable.

Comparison

Current store checks

Store adapters return only prices they can verify. Each result links directly to the retailer listing or search page.

History

Recorded observations

Price history is built from successful checks stored in Supabase. A buy-or-wait label is shown only when there is history across multiple days.

Catalog

Editorial details

Product names, brands, GadgetVerse reviews, and ratings come from the database catalog. Prices are not part of the catalog seed.

Integrity & data handling

How we handle prices & your data

GadgetVerse is designed around consumer transparency: no invented discounts, no tracking bloat, and no fake urgency.

No Fabricated Prices

Every price is queried live or retrieved from logged observations. If a store is unconfigured or unavailable, we display it honestly.

Target Price Privacy

Alert emails are stored solely to deliver your requested price drop notification. Never sold or shared with advertiser networks.

Direct Retailer Links

Store links navigate directly to verified retailer search or product pages without misleading intermediate ad networks.

Verifiable Timestamps

Every store offer displays the exact timestamp it was observed so you always know how fresh the data is.
